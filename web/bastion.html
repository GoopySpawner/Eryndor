Veil Expansion

Spend 1 Sanctified Residuum to expand. Expansion is irreversible and permanently adds +1 Attacker Die to every Bastion Event Attack.

Viremont frontier doctrine: a Bastion is a stabilized area in the Veil, maintained by Hollowed Residuum that fuels an Omen Machine. Facilities may not exceed current capacity; newly gained slots may be assigned immediately or kept in reserve.

Expansion permanently displaces the Veil boundary. Field records associate each expansion with greater Hollow attention: the larger the Bastion becomes, the more aggressively the Hollow behaves nearby.

One week of Bastion management

Bastion Turn

    Current turn log

      Bastion resource reference

      Bastion Rations: Food for defenders; spend 1 during each Upkeep. Missing rations reduce Morale.

      Munitions: Spend during an attack to add 1 per Defender to Defender Value.

      Bastion Defenders: Roll one Defender Die per Defender during an attack.

      Materials: Used to repair one destroyed facility for 1 Material during Upkeep.

      Gold: Tracked without a facility storage cap.

      Morale: −5 to +5. Natural Morale Decay cannot go below 0; external effects can. Morale modifies production rolls.

      Hollowed Residuum: Spend 1 during Upkeep to maintain the Bastion border from the Veil. Missing it lowers Defender Dice one size for that turn.

      Sanctified Residuum: Made by converting 20 Hollowed Residuum in a Residuum Crucible; spend 1 to expand the Bastion.

      Each operational Roomy Garden, Barracks, Storehouse, and Armory stores up to 10 of its resource. A Vast version stores up to 25 and rolls twice. Root Cellar, Bunk Room, Powder Storage, and Materials Closet each add 5 to their matching capacity. Excess above capacity is removed at the start of the next Bastion Turn.

      Facility reference

      Test setup capacity: 6 Cramped, 4 Roomy, and 1 Vast slot. The Garden occupies one of the four Roomy slots.

      Facility sizes

      Cramped: Auxiliary structures; typically the first destroyed.

      Roomy: Core operations and logistics.

      Vast: Defining, costly structures with major benefits.

      Bastion Turn steps

      1. Upkeep: Spend 1 Ration and 1 Hollowed Residuum. Missing Rations decrease Morale by 1; missing Residuum reduces Defender Dice one size. Repair 1 destroyed facility for 1 Material. Remove resources over their storage capacity.
      2. Morale Decay: Reduce Morale by 1; natural decay cannot take it below 0.
      3. Resource Production: Each operational Garden, Barracks, Storehouse, or Armory rolls 1d6 and adds Morale. Vast producers roll twice separately. Modified result ≤2 produces 0; 3–4 produces 1; 5–6 produces 2; 7+ produces 3. The Coal Furnace can add +1 to each production roll for 1 Material.
      4. Facility Operations: Resolve normal facility functions.
      5. Roll Event: Roll 1d20 on the event table and roll its Hollow attacker dice.
      6. Apply Event Effects: Resolve the defense, resource changes, defender losses, and facility damage.
      7. Prepare for Next Week: Review facility status and prepare the Bastion for the next turn.
      8. End Turn: State carries over to the next week.

      Defense: Attacker Value is the attacker-dice total. Defender Value is Defender Dice + applicable facility bonuses + optional Munitions (1 per Defender for each Munition). X = floor((Attacker Value − Defender Value) / 2). X ≤ 0 is a success; X ≥ 1 is a failure. On failure, lose X Defenders; if X exceeds the Defender count, destroy X − Defender count facilities. Facility destruction prioritizes Walls/Watchtower, then Cramped, Roomy, and Vast.

      The tracker uses round-down for X as requested. Choose event resource effects and facility losses before they are applied. The full Bastion tracker is available to anyone with the party-page password.
